# Code of conduct

Syncwave is about listening to music with other people, and the project should feel the same way.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people asking questions are just stuck.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Respect that contributors give their time for free.
- Keep discussion on topic.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or jokes of any kind.
- Posting someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break these rules. Serious or repeated cases lead to a permanent ban from the project.

To report something, email **hello@awetomiq.com**. Reports are kept confidential.
