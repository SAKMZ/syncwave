Listen together, in perfect sync.

Start a room, send one link, and everyone hears the same second of the same song. A shared queue, live chat and reactions, with no accounts and no Premium. It runs on a computer you already own.

What's in a room

Everything a listening party needs, nothing it doesn't.

Think house party, long-distance date night, study room, or the music channel of your Discord, except everyone hears it at full quality and can actually run the queue.

Playback that stays locked

The server keeps the clock and every listener follows it. Nobody drifts, and a laptop waking from sleep catches up instead of dragging the room back.

One link, no sign-ups

Share a code or a URL. Friends pick a name and they're in. Rooms survive restarts, so a link can stay your group's spot for good.

A queue people can share

Anyone can add. Upvotes move a track one place per person, so one enthusiast can't take over. Drag to reorder, vote to skip, and see who added what.

Chat, reactions, presence

See who's typing, queueing or away. Emoji reactions burst identically on every screen, and joins and skips go to their own feed so they don't bury the chat.

An optional AI DJ

Type /dj ten rainy-day songs in chat and it fills the queue, then introduces tracks in character. Works with a free Gemini key or a local Ollama. Off by default.

Made for phones

Swipeable panes, lock-screen controls, and installable to the home screen. Crossfade, volume levelling and casting to your speakers are built in.

Run your own

Your server, your music, your rules.

There's no hosted service to sign up for. Syncwave runs on your machine, and your friends connect to it.

Easiest

Double-click it

Download, unzip, and run start.bat on Windows or start.sh on macOS and Linux. It fetches what it needs, opens your browser, and prints a public link you can send to anyone.

Always-on box

Docker

For a home server or a NAS that stays on.

git clone https://github.com/SAKMZ/syncwave.git
cd syncwave && cp .env.example .env
docker compose up -d --build
Linux server

One command

Installs everything on Ubuntu or Debian. Add a domain and it sets up HTTPS for you.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SAKMZ/syncwave/main/scripts/install.sh | sudo bash

Hosting on a cloud VPS? YouTube refuses most datacenter IPs, so you'll need a proxy pool or a cookies file. It's one setting in the admin console, and the self-host guide walks through it.

Questions

The ones people actually ask.

Does everyone need Spotify, or Premium?

No. Nobody needs an account with anything. Audio is fetched by your server and streamed from it, so listeners just open a link.

How many people can join a room?

There's no built-in cap. The real limit is your upload speed, since each listener streams from your machine. A group of friends on a home connection is comfortable.

Can I use it alongside Discord?

That's the most common setup: voice in Discord, music in a Syncwave room. Unlike a music bot, everyone hears full quality and can queue, vote and see what's playing.

Do I have to pay for the AI DJ?

No. Run it on your own machine with Ollama and no key at all, or use a free Gemini key from Google. It's off until you turn it on, and your key never leaves your server.

Where does the music come from?

YouTube Music, fetched with yt-dlp and cached on your disk. Syncwave is a self-host tool for you and your friends; please don't run it as a public or paid service.

Is it free?

Free to run, fork and change for personal, hobby and nonprofit use, under the PolyForm Noncommercial license. Selling access or bundling it into a paid product needs a separate agreement. Get in touch if that's you.
